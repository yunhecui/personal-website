Selected Work

Case studies spanning spatial ML, optimization, and mobility research.

Research

Urban Micro-Freight Optimization

Formulated urban micro-freight allocation as a multi-objective constrained optimization problem; engineered a demand-supply matching pipeline with PyTorch-based modules to improve delivery efficiency and energy consumption. (NREL)

Deep Learning

GNN Mobility Forecasting

Built a graph neural network using PyTorch Geometric to predict MEP scores in data-sparse cities, enabling scalable estimation across 100+ metropolitan areas by learning latent mobility patterns from network topology and demographics. (NREL)

Featured · Applied ML

New Build Branch Model

A spatial ML solution forecasting deposit performance for newly built branches. Directly informs Chase's branch expansion strategy and achieves a ~70% reduction in forecast error, improving capital allocation decisions. (JPMorgan Chase)

Publication

FlowHC: Flow Trend Extraction

A method for extracting major flow trends from broad-scale mobility data. Presented at the Transportation Research Board 105th Annual Meeting (2025).

Publication

NYC Dock-Based Bike-Share Study

Explored whether dock-based bike-sharing competes with, integrates with, or complements transit in New York City. Published in The Professional Geographer (2023).

Research

CTfastrak Mobility Metrics Framework

Designed a metrics framework with custom KPIs and causal inference methods to quantify urban mobility patterns; analysis drove $1.2M in resource allocation decisions for the CTfastrak transit system. (UConn)
